If \(z{\lt}y\) and every corrected candidate complement is below \(y^3\), then \(|\mathcal C(N)|-(P(N)+T(N))/2\le |\mathcal C(N)\cap G(N)|\). The proof sums the pointwise weight bound.

Theorem 34Chen: 0.67 representation bound

For every sufficiently large even \(N\), \(0.67\, \mathfrak S_{\mathrm{Liu}}(N)N/(\log N)^2\le |G(N)|\), where \(G(N)\) is the original set of primes \(p{\lt}N\) with \(N-p\ge 2\) and at most two prime factors counted with multiplicity.

Theorem 4Bombieri–Vinogradov for primes

For every \(A{\gt}0\) there are \(B\ge 0\), \(C{\gt}0\) and \(N_0\) such that \(\sum _{1\le q\le Q_B(N)}E^*(N,q)\le CN/(\log N)^A\) for integers \(N\ge N_0\), where \(Q_B(N)=\lfloor N^{1/2}/(\log N)^B\rfloor \) and \(E^*(N,q)=\max _{y\in \{ 0,\ldots ,N\} }\max _{0\le a{\lt}q,\, (a,q)=1}|\pi (y;q,a)-L_*(y)/\varphi (q)|\). Here \(L_*(x)=2/\log 2+\int _2^xdt/\log t\) for \(x{\gt}1\), while \(L_*(0)=L_*(1)=2/\log 2\) by the totalized interval-integral convention, not a principal value; \(E^*(N,0)=0\).

\(\exists K\in \mathbb N\), \(4\le K\), \(\forall N\ge K\), \(2\mid N\Rightarrow (1/2500)\mathcal X_N{\lt}D_{1,19/10}(N)\). Here \(D\) counts distinct primes \(p\) and \(\mathcal X_N=\mathfrak S_{\rm Liu}(N)N/\log ^2N\), with no extra factor two.
