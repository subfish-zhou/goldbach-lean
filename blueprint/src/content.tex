\chapter{The public proof boundary}

Chen's theorem asserts that every sufficiently large even natural number is
a prime plus either a prime or a product of two primes. This does not assert
the binary Goldbach conjecture or an explicit numerical threshold.

This blueprint shows seven selected declarations, not every lemma in the
project. Its six displayed edges are inferred by LeanArchitect from the compiled
endpoint declarations. The first three input nodes are explicit graph boundaries:
their upstream proof dependencies are omitted from this view, not from Lean.
The prose summaries accompany, rather than replace, the exact Lean statements.
Both endpoint routes consume the same proved distribution input; the public
existence theorem does not directly call the public quantitative theorem.
