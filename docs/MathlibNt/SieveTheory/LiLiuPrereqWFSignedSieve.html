    Finite sieve inequalities for the fixed normalized signed family #

    The full-integer family is not squarefree-masked. Consequently its sieve inequalities are stated over divisors of the sieve primorial (or its gcd with an arbitrary integer), not over unrestricted divisors.

    We first sum the actual small Rosser weights on the positive and negative rough branches separately. Only then do we use the normalized rough sieve inequalities. The tight lower-even convention remains that of (18).
