    ℝ

    The unnormalised upper-Rosser boundary layer at pair depth k. The distinguished terminal prime is external to the stored even chain, so this is the odd Suzuki source layer 2*k+1. Its terminal factor is the Euler product strictly below q, not an inverse suffix product.
