    Actual signed remainders for the common normalized family #

    The sequence keeps its indices, so equal integers retain their multiplicity. All divisor sums are over the sieve primorial. The remainder is the actual divisibility count minus the chosen main density, never an assumed error certificate and never a sum of absolute values.
