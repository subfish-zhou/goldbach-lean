    Liu's Selberg assembly on the even filter #

    This module instantiates the source-facing Selberg argument with the explicit optimizer liuSelbergOptimalLambda N epsilon. Evenness is carried by the filter rather than requested for all natural numbers. It retains the componentwise source-input route and also supplies the canonical aggregate Pan--Wang--Ding route.
