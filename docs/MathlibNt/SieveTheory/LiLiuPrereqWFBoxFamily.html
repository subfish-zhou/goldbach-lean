    Fixed multi-box weights at every real level split #

    The hypothesis is the explicit numerical prefix-square condition on the list of actual prime upper bounds. Repeated labels retain their slot multiplicity. The subsequent LiLiuPrereqWFGeometry and LiLiuPrereqWFGeometricBoxes derive this condition with Iwaniec's geometric parameter losses. Constructing the complete signed sieve family with its density remains a separate task.
