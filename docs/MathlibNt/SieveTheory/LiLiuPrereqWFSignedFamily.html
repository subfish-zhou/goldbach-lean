    A fixed occurrence-aware normalized signed geometric family #

    The index set is an image of a powerset, so one multiplicity profile occurs once, regardless of how many prime subsets realize it. Each member is one full-integer divided-power box product with the actual small Rosser weight. The family, the sign, and the small weight are fixed before every level split.

    The favourable signs use lower endpoints; the opposite signs require distinct boxes and the strict upper-endpoint tests. This is the normalized convention of SignedRounding, not Iwaniec's factorial-copy labelled-slot sum.
