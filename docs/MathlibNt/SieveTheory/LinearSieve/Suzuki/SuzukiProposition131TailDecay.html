    Proposition 13.1(iii): quantitative hat-tail decay #

    Section13HatContract records the DDE, initial values, positivity, and only the qualitative limit weightedHat → 0. The missing publication interface is a quantitative modulus for the adjacent tail ratio. The upstream contract below records the source-strength (M log (eM))⁻² estimate at M; monotonicity from the DDE then compares M with every s ≤ M. Thus this is not the downstream consumer contract (which has already forgotten the logarithmic gain).
