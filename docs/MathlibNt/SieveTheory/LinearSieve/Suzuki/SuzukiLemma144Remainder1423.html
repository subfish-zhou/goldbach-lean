    Case I, (14.23): source-faithful remainder interface #

    The paper does not replace the two Lemma-8.7 endpoints by an arbitrary fixed quotient. Claim 14.6(ii) licenses Lemma 8.7, Claim 14.6(iii) supplies the main contraction, and the two remaining endpoint terms retain the literal coefficient 3 * (κ + 1) * K^2, hence 6 * K^2 in the production specialization κ = 1.

    The paper's subsequent estimates contain σ^3 log (e σ). Consequently the already accepted σ^2 scalar decay is necessary normalization data but is not, by itself, the source estimate. The predicates below freeze the exact additional source inequalities without choosing numerical constants hidden by the paper's O notation.
