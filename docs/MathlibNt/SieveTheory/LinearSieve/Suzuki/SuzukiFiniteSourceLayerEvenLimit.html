    The even continuous-layer limit at the lower source #

    The available source theory proves nonnegativity of every dimension-one layer, but does not yet prove summability of the lower even layers. Accordingly this module defines the unconditional limit honestly in ℝ≥0∞, as the supremum of the even partial sums. This allows the production depth to vary with the finite carrier: no fixed-depth diagonal argument is used.
