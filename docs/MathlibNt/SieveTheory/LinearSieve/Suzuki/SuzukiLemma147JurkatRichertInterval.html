    Suzuki Lemma 14.7 on the Jurkat--Richert interval #

    This module passes from the exact finite lower factor supplied by the literal all-depth theorem to Suzuki's canonical continuous factor. The comparison uses only nonnegativity and summability of the even source layers, so it is valid on the full legal range 2 ≤ s. On 4 ≤ s ≤ 6, the source normalization theorem then identifies that factor with the standard dimension-one Jurkat--Richert factor. The adaptive depth and the complete error term are unchanged.
