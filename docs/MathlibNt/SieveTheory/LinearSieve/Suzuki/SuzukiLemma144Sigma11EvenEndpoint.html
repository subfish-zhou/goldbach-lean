    Lemma 14.4: the even Σ₁₁ endpoint #

    At even depth M, the predecessor depth M-1 is odd. Its source domain is open at 1, but the κ=1 layers are regular on the closed envelope. We use that closed-envelope regularity only to run Lemma 8.7 and the finite recursion at the endpoint. The prime carrier itself is strict, so no prime coordinate is added.
