    Real convergence of the even lower source layers #

    This module turns the all-depth source/hat comparison proved in the production Lemma 13.2 development into genuine real summability. In particular, the ENNReal supremum used by SuzukiFiniteSourceLayerEvenLimit is finite whenever the production Section-13 source contract is available. No finite scan and no caller-supplied summable sequence occurs here.
