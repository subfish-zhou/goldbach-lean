    The Σ₁₂ endpoint q_D factor #

    The literal factor-one predicate in CaseISigma12QDEnvelopeFactor is false for arbitrary Section13HatLayers: its structure carries no relation between the two signed layers. The endpoint argument only needs a factor fixed in D. The result below supplies exactly that factor from the positive Section-13 contract; in particular the desired comparison is not assumed as a premise.
