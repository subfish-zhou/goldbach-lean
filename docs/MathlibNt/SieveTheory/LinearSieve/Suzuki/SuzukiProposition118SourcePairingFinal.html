    Final source-pairing closure for Suzuki Proposition 11.8 #

    The genuine source pairing is continuous on every [2,M] and has zero right derivative at every point of [2,M), including the lower endpoint 2 and the kink 3. The right-derivative constant theorem therefore proves conservation on the whole closed interval in one step. Decay at infinity then forces the boundary scalar, and hence Suzuki's B, to vanish.
