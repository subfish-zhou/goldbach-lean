    Uniform stationary exclusion and non-circular minus-parameter selection #

    The key input from source Lemma 10.27 is kept as an adjacent-value comparison, not as the desired scalar inequality. Division by the positive s * R s and the stationary equation give (10.46), namely ξ(s)-c-2/s = R(s-1)/(sR(s)) ≥ 1-K/s. Consequently the cutoff below depends on the comparison constants (and hence on R) but not on c.
