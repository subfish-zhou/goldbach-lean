    Jurkat--Richert (5.10) for the constructed global delay functions #

    The common limit is 1: pair the actual sum F + f with the independently constructed Laplace adjoint. The conserved pairing is 2 at the initial endpoint and twice the common limit at infinity.

    This is a modern adjoint proof of the exact statement on printed p. 226, not the original paper's appeal to de Bruijn's estimates (5.3). Only the generic pairing identity and unconditional adjoint producers are reused; no Suzuki source-tail or amplitude-normalization hypothesis is consumed.
