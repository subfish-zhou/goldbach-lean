    Richert 1969, hypothesis (A4) and the squareful payment #

    Frozen source:

    • references/richert-1969/richert-1969.pdf, PDF pages 2 and 10, hypothesis (A4) and equation (3.7);
    • references/richert-1969/VISION_TRANSCRIPTION.md, sections 1 and 5.

    This file identifies the carrier removed by the prime on Richert's weighted sum with a finite union of the p^2 ∣ a fibres. Hypothesis (A4) is then applied fibre by fibre. No sieve fundamental lemma is used here.
