    Richert 1969: specialization of ordinary (4.18) to Chen's weighted remainder #

    Frozen source:

    • references/richert-1969/richert-1969.pdf, PDF pages 17--20, equation (4.18) and the Cauchy--Schwarz argument after (4.22);
    • references/richert-1969/VISION_TRANSCRIPTION.md, sections 7 and 9.

    This module specializes the quantified ordinary prefix-maximal Bombieri--Vinogradov estimate to Chen's combined moduli. The cutoff, pointwise envelope, Lemma 3 payment, and logarithmic-integral normalization shift are all discharged here. No weighted Bombieri theorem is assumed.
