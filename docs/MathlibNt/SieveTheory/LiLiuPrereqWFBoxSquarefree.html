    Exact squarefree coefficients of the fixed divided-power box #

    The existing full-integer weight is not changed. On a squarefree integer with k prime factors in the box, the raw slot sum is exactly k! and its divided power is exactly one. This distinguishes a normalized aggregate from its factorial-copy expansion; it asserts no sieve-density estimate.
