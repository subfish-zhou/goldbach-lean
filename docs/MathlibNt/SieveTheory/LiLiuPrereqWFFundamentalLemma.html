    Large-D fundamental-lemma density of the original small weights #

    The actual JR/Suzuki estimate controls the entire defects, including their low-prime contributions. An absolute constant is selected before ε; the explicit threshold is selected before all sieve data, K, and depths. This proves the large-D form of Iwaniec p.316 (22), not the density of the as-yet unassembled full signed box family.
