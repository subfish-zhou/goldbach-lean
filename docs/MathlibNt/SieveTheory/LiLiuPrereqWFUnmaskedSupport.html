    The actual non-squarefree support of the unmasked family #

    The small Rosser factor is squarefree and its prime range is disjoint from every large box. Consequently a repeated prime in a nonzero coefficient must be a rough prime. This is a statement about the original full-integer weights; no squarefree mask is applied and no preservation of well-factorability by a mask is asserted.
