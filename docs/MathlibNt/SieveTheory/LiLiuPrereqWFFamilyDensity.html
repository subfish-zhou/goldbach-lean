    Genuine F/f density of the fixed normalized signed family #

    All three terms in the comparison use the same coefficients. In particular, the lower small-weight density is never assumed positive: its error is paid against the absolute rough Euler majorant before the coarse F/f inequalities are used. The original dimension-one constant and uniform threshold order are retained.
