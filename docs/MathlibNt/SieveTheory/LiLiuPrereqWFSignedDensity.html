    Same-family density and the small-weight replacement cost #

    All densities below belong to the fixed normalized family of SignedFamily. The negative rough mass is counted once per prime subset, not once per labelled permutation. The upper replacement error is added, and the lower replacement error is subtracted. The remaining coarse rounded density is not estimated by the small-weight fundamental lemma.
