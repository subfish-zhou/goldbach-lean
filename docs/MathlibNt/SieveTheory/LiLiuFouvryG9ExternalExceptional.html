    Exceptional mass and finite remainder transport for the actual external family #

    All coefficients below are the original full-integer externalTerm weights. The upper edge uses a smaller prime carrier only in the existing family definition; exceptional moduli are still measured against the original primorial. The generic finite remainder budget does not assume injectivity of any underlying sequence.
