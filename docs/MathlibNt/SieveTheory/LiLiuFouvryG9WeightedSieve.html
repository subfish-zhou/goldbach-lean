    Nonnegative weighted finite sieve for the existing external WF family #

    Indices are retained: entries may be zero or repeated, and no injectivity hypothesis is imposed. Only the nonnegative sequence weights multiply an inequality. The signed external coefficients are rearranged by exact finite sum identities. All divisor sums remain over the original primorial; no transport to a full level interval or analytic density estimate is claimed.
