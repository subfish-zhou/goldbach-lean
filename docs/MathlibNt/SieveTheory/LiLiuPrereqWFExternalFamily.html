    Fixed piecewise families at the external edge #

    Above sqrt D the lower family is a singleton zero weight and the upper family is the original family on primes below sqrt D. No coefficients are masked. Both the density and the signed remainder below sum the actual members over divisors of the ORIGINAL primorial.
