    Rosser lower sieves and Mertens normalization #

    Depth-zero boundary assembly, lower Rosser density inputs, and standard Bombieri--Vinogradov distribution bounds combine with explicit Mertens and singular-series normalization to give the base lower-sieve asymptotic.

    All declarations retain the MathlibNt.SieveTheory.SwitchingPrinciple namespace.
