    Varying-prime sieves and cutoff corrections #

    Upper Rosser certificates and weighted Bombieri--Vinogradov remainders control the medium-prime aggregate. Nonreduced residues and cutoff fibers are bounded explicitly before comparison with the corrected weighted count.
