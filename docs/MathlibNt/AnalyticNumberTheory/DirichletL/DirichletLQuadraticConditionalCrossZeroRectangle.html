    A fixed quadratic rectangle crossing height zero #

    The high-height quadratic argument contains 1 / |2t|, so its fixed-height majorant degenerates as t → 0. Here the low two-segment argument is kept separate. A fixed conductor cutoff at height T, paid for directly by the raw lower bound for L(1, χ), supplies a positive central band. The existing annular rectangles cover the two remaining bands. Their common left edge then gives one rectangle across the whole interval [-T,T].
