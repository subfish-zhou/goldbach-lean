    A logarithmic conductor-height bound for nonprincipal Dirichlet L-values #

    The natural-order conditional series is cut at q * (⌊|t|⌋₊ + 1). The finite part is bounded by a harmonic sum, while the factor q / m in the Abel tail pays for the height of s.
