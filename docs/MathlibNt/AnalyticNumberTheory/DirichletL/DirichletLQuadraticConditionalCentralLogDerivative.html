    Quantitative logarithmic derivative in the quadratic central band #

    This file turns the raw lower bound at s = 1 into an explicit lower bound throughout the central band used by the cross-zero rectangle. In particular, the denominator in L'/L is paid quantitatively; zero-freeness or compactness alone is never used as a substitute for a uniform lower bound.

    The resulting bound retains the honest factor q^η / c forced by the supplied raw Siegel lower bound c q⁻η ≤ L(1,χ). Removing that power requires a stronger (polylogarithmic) lower input, not a topological argument.
