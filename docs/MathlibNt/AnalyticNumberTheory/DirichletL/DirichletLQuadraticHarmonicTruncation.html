    Real harmonic truncations for quadratic Dirichlet L-values #

    This module turns the natural-order Abel theory into a finite, real-valued approximation to L(1, χ). It is the first elementary reduction used in the large-conductor Landau--Siegel argument: after this point the missing input is a lower bound for an explicit finite quadratic-character harmonic sum.

    The real harmonic truncation of a Dirichlet character at m. Keeping the finite sum in cpowWeight form makes its relation with the already established natural-order conditional series literal.
