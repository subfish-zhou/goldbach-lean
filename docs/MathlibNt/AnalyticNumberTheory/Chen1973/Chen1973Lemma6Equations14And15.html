    Chen 1973, Lemma 6, equations (14) and (15) #

    This file freezes the finite algebra on p. 120. All Dirichlet polynomials use natural order. In particular, no conditionally convergent tsum is used. Equation (14) is separated into its finite large-sieve term and the literal truncation remainder. Equation (15) is the fourth moment of the finite Möbius polynomial. The final two results record the actual unconditional Lemma 3 calls needed on the adjacent Cauchy circle.
