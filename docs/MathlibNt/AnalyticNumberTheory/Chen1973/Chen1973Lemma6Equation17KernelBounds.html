    This independent leaf records the literal 11/10 Perron scale, pointwise Cauchy decay of the denominator on both source lines, its half-line integral, and the elementary alpha/beta, reciprocal-log, and conductor-weight payments. All cutoffs and constants are explicit; no finite computation is used.
