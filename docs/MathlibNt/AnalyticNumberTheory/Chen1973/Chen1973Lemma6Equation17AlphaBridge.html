    Chen 1973, Lemma 6, equation (17): alpha-line log-derivative bridge #

    This module moves the exact finite ActualPhi identity from the unconditional Re s = 2 Bromwich representation to Chen's line Re s = alpha = 1 + 1 / log x, identifies the von Mangoldt series with -L'/L, and lifts the identity through the finite pair, primitive-character, and conductor-block sums. It does not assume an equation-(17) majorization or any later assembly conclusion.
