    Chen 1973, Lemma 6, equation (17): finite Bromwich sums #

    This module closes the finite-sum part of the passage to (17). It proves that Chen's actual finite Φ is a Bochner integral over the full real Bromwich line, exchanges that integral with the finite von-Mangoldt, prime-pair, and primitive character sums, and then records the resulting exact conductor-block identity. No equation-(17) majorization or contour-deformation conclusion is assumed.
