    Chen 1973, Lemma 6, equation (19): moment transport #

    This leaf pays the change from the squarefree equation-(17) weight |μ(d)| 3^ω(d) / d to the reciprocal-totient weight used in (14), (15), and Lemma 2. It also applies the sharp, unconditional Lemma 2 to the literal prime-pair polynomial. The bounds stop at scalar moments and do not assume the final cell estimate.
