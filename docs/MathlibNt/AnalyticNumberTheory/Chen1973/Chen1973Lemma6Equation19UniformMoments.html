    Chen 1973, Lemma 6, equation (19): uniform moment constants #

    This leaf freezes the sharp Lemma-2 constant before every coefficient, spectral height, and cell parameter. It also records injectivity of the actual ordered prime-pair shell, the structural input needed to pay its collected coefficient energy without a collision multiplicity. No final-cell estimate is assumed.
