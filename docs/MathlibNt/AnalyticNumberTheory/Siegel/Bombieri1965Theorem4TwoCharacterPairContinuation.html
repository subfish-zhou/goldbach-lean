    Mellin continuation of a nonprincipal character pair #

    This modern analytic argument identifies the Mellin integral of the actual convolution of two nonprincipal characters with the product of their L-functions on Re s > 1/2. No primitivity or quadraticity is needed. In particular the value at one is the actual L-value product, not a formal or absolutely convergent harmonic series.
