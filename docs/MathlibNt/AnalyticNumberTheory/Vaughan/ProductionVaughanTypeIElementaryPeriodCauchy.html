    Elementary-period Vaughan Type-I bound by row Cauchy #

    This checker keeps the literal first and middle Vaughan long-row identities. Inside either lane it uses finite Cauchy--Schwarz on the complete row index; there is no rowwise triangle inequality. The sole lane triangle is applied to first - middle after both complete lanes have been bounded.
