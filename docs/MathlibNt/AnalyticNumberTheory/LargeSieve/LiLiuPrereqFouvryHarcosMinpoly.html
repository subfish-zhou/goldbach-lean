    Minimal-polynomial linkage in Harcos's Theorem 6 #

    Harcos, §3, pages 7–8 (pages/harcos-lpolynomial-07.png and pages/harcos-stepanov-08.png), identifies the character on each orbit with a power of the polynomial character. The extension multiplicity is a natural-number quotient, not division in the base field.
