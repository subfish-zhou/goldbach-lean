    The beta-pair mass discarded at a large common divisor #

    Here the common divisor is gcd(n₁, n₂) of the two beta variables, not gcd(q, r) of the moduli. The elementary gcd mean and the global second divisor moment give an explicit inverse-square-root cutoff gain. No arithmetic-progression, Siegel--Walfisz, or Shiu estimate is used.
