    Analytic reduction of the original C.2 error on its full modulus range #

    The well-factorable factors are selected before the varying residue. The dyadic block is selected afterwards from the actual retained frequencies. Neither the first signed coefficient nor the real Fourier factor is replaced by a pointwise absolute-value majorant.
