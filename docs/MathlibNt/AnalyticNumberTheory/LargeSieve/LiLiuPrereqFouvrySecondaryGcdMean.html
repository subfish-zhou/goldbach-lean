    Removing the artificial progression-step loss in the secondary mean #

    The previous inclusion of multiples in (0,B*s] paid a full factor s. The gcd product inequality instead pays only gcd(s,|A|). No coprimality of s and A, and no nonzero coefficient of the common n, is assumed.
