    The zero mode of the large beta-gcd exclusion #

    The bound applies to every further submask of gcd(n₁,n₂)>Y. It is an absolute zero-mode bound, not a restriction of the signed unmasked Siegel--Walfisz cancellation. The original progression sum and the other four gcd exclusions still require separate estimates.
