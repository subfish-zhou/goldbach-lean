    The small-modulus root of the W phase #

    Fouvry (1984), p. 237, (8.8), and Fouvry (1987), p. 627, (3.11). The large CRT residue may be replaced, modulo D, by the CRT on the two small moduli. The resulting root phase is constant on explicit congruence classes; no equality of chosen integer representatives is asserted.
