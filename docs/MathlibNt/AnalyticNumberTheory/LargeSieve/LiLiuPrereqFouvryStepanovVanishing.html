    Vanishing of the actual Stepanov auxiliary polynomial #

    This is the passage from (20) to (22) on printed page 11 of Harcos, Weil's bound for Kloosterman sums (pages/harcos-stepanov-11.png). Frobenius kills the positive Hasse derivatives of X^(jq) below order q. Factoring out f^(ell-k) additionally requires k ≤ ell; the vanishing application uses k < ell ≤ q.

    (Polynomial.hasseDeriv k) (Polynomial.X ^ (j * Fintype.card F)) = 0

    Frobenius forces every positive Hasse derivative of X^(jq) below order q to vanish as a polynomial, not just as a function on the field. The Frobenius residue of ((X + 1)^j)^q determines its low Taylor coefficients; evaluating a Hasse monomial at 1 recovers its sole coefficient.
