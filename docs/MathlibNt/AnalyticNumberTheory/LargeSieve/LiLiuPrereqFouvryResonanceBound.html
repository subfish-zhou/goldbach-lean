    Uniform local-scale payment for the resonance divisor count #

    The proven pointwise divisor estimate pays both finite divisor choices. The bounds are uniform in the common first beta index and signed product. A bounds |P|, and B bounds d₁*n + |P|; no dyadic parameters are confused with the original, unextracted beta coordinates.
