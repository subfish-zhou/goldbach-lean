    An actual W phase: CRT peeling and reciprocal inversion #

    This is the first reciprocal step of Fouvry (1984), p. 238, (8.14), applied to the existing product CRT residue. The moduli q,r need not be coprime. The peeled factor k is coprime to its complement in their lcm. No five-gcd decomposition, small-modulus bound, or estimate for the finite remainder is asserted here. In particular the complementary phase has not been discarded.
