    Reciprocal cancellation on unit-step arithmetic progressions #

    The step is a unit modulo the modulus, not necessarily one as an integer. The integer parameter retains its multiplicity on intervals longer than the modulus. Only the inherent nonunit vanishing of reciprocalPhase is imposed.
