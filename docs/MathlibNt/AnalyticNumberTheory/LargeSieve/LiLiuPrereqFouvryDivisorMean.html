    Fixed-order divisor means and coefficient square sums #

    The actual ordered divisor function is the Dirichlet convolution power ζ ^ k. The mean estimate uses only the elementary hyperbola identity and the harmonic sum bound. The square majorant is unrestricted: no squarefree support is needed.
