    A signed factorization and its high-omega deletion #

    The original weight is well-factorable at its original level, with closed factor supports. Only one legitimate split is chosen. Deleting high omega from the second factor produces a convolution, not a new well-factorable weight. Its discarded part has an actual divisor majorant and high-omega modulus support.
