    Twisted Chinese remaindering for the actual complete Kloosterman sum #

    The two twists are the integer Bezout coefficients: m * gcdA m n + n * gcdB m n = 1. Thus gcdB m n is the inverse of n modulo m. No frequency is required to be a unit, and either coprime factor may be one. These are exact arithmetic identities, not an invocation of a Weil bound.
