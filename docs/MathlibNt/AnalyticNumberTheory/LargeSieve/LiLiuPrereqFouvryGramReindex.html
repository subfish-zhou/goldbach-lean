    Occupied labels for the entire separated Gram energy #

    A label retains the common (r,n₁) and both ordered triples (n₂,s,h). It forgets only the common k, which is summed inside its fiber. Labels are images of actual tuple-pairs, not an unrestricted rectangular box.
