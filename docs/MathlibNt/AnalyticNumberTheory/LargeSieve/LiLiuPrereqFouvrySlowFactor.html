    The concrete five-variable slow factor, in logarithmic coordinates #

    The coordinate order is (h,k,n,r,s). Logarithmic coordinates are convenient for dyadic partial summation: their interval lengths are at most log 2, independently of the original arithmetic scales. The two real phase parameters include all scaling constants; arithmetic coefficients and reciprocal/root phases are not part of this weight.
