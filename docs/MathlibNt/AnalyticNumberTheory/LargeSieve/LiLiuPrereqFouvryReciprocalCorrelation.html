    The same-first-index reciprocal correlation in F87 IV.3 #

    Fouvry (1987), p. 632, (4.7)--(4.8). The two phases share n, not just d₁ and r. Their common modulus is n*r*s*s', with no second copy of n. The factors s,s' need not be coprime. Signed frequencies and residue, zero correlation numerator, and modulus one are all retained.
