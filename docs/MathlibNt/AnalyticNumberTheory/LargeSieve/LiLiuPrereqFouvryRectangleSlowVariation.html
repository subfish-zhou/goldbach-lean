    Cardinality-free partial summation for the coupled five-variable weight #

    The corner stencil is identified with the genuine mixed increments of the concrete normalized weight. Interior grid widths telescope, and upper faces contribute one endpoint mass per inactive coordinate. The final factor is at most 2^5; no arithmetic coefficient or mask is differentiated.
