    One coprimality sieve on a unit residue-class interval #

    Finite Mobius inversion costs only the divisors of the specified sieve integer. No coprimality between that integer and either modulus is assumed: divisors meeting the phase modulus vanish intrinsically, and divisors meeting the progression modulus are excluded by the unit residue. The surviving divisor sections are CRT progressions of step e * v.

        Cancellation on one genuine sieved residue interval.

        The constant depends only on ε. In particular, A need not be coprime to q or v. The divisor cost is only A.divisors.card; the original interval span is divided by v*q, with no loss of the progression saving.
