    Full zero-mode estimate with the lcm weight retained #

    The all-positive-modulus beta-AP input in F87 (1.3) is applied at the actual gcd, without a small/large gcd split. A fixed-order divisor majorant and the harmonic lcm mean pay all modulus weights. This estimates the full unpruned W zero mode minus U zero mode, not the nonzero Fourier remainder.
