    Family-uniform logarithmic payment of the complete zero-mode difference #

    The SW input is the existing all-positive-modulus, coprime-sieved family condition. Constants precede all family members, supports, scales, modulus weights and integer residues. The lcm-weight estimate leaves no gcd range unpaid in W zero minus U zero. Nonzero W frequencies are not estimated here.
