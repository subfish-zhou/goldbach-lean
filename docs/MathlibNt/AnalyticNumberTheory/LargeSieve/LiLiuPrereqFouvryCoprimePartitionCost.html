    Subpolynomial cost at the F87 prime-factor cutoff #

    The partition count is at most x^ε, uniformly for 2 ≤ T ≤ x and ω ≤ C*(log x)^(1/5), for any fixed positive C. In particular C = 2 includes the product-variable cutoff used in F87, Section III.7, p. 628.

    The asymptotic cost is uniform both in the real cutoff and in the growing number of distinct prime factors. No fixed-ω assumption is used.
