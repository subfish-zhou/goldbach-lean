    Finite power sums detect the modulus of every root #

    This supplies the growth-to-root-modulus implication used on page 1 of Harcos, Weil's bound for Kloosterman sums (pages/harcos-weil-01.png). Cesàro averages isolate all copies of a maximal-modulus root simultaneously; their positive multiplicity prevents cancellation. No distinctness is needed.
