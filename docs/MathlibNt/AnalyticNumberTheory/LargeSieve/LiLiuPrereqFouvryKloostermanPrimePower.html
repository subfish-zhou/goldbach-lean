    Elementary prime-power cancellation #

    Translation by the last nonzero prime-power layer preserves units. If just one frequency is divisible by the prime, it multiplies the complete sum by a nontrivial constant character, forcing the sum to vanish. The argument works in characteristic two as well; it uses no quadratic Gauss-sum formula.
