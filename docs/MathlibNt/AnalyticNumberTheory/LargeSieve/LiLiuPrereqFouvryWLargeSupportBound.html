    Original and zero-mode bounds for a large supported beta factor #

    The canonical d₁ condition gives a sparse square-divisor strip in the first beta coordinate. Cauchy--Schwarz then saves a fourth root of the cutoff. The original and zero-mode estimates apply to the identical arbitrary mask; no symmetry or preservation of well-factorability under masking is assumed.
