    Sparse-modulus exclusion in the original progression sum #

    The elementary progression-counting part of Fouvry (1984), p. 236, (8.5). Coprimality is retained in the sparse modulus row until the actual bump progression is counted. Absolute values are taken before enlarging any mask.
