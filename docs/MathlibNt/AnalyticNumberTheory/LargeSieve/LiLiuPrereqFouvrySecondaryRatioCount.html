    Counting the secondary frequency-sieve ratio relation #

    For fixed positive s, the equation h'*s=h*s' forces s' to be a multiple of s/gcd(s,h) and determines h' uniquely. In a dyadic s block this pays only 2*gcd(s,h) partners, not an independent frequency and sieve-variable box. The resulting collision count is logarithmic.
