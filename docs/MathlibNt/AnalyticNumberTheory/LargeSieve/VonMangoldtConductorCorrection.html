    Von Mangoldt conductor change-level correction #

    For the coefficient Λ(n) on the integer interval [1,N], the terms on which changing a Dirichlet character from level q to its conductor can disagree are prime powers p^k with p ∣ q. This gives a polylogarithmic bound for every correction prefix and, after summing over levels and nonprincipal characters, a Q^2 (rather than N-times-energy) correction.

    The interval starts at zero: all prefix sums below are over [1,y]. No claim is made here for translated intervals, where a separate count of prime powers in a short interval would be needed.
