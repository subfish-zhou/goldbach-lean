    Small gcd contribution to the actual W-minus-U zero mode #

    The beta input is an independent coprime-sieved AP discrepancy estimate. An L-infinity times L-one covariance bound avoids a factor of the gcd. Elementary reciprocal divisor means then evaluate the entire small-gcd modulus sum. The complementary large-gcd contribution is left explicit.
