    The coprimality partition of Fouvry #

    The binary separation argument underlying F84, Lemma 6 (pp. 226–227), and F87, Lemma 7 (p. 624). The exponent is the square of the bound on the number of distinct prime factors, as in F84's proof and F87's statement.
