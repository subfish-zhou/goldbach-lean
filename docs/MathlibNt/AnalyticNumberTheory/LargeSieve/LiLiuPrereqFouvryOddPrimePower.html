    Primitive Weil bounds for every power of an odd prime #

    The prime endpoint uses the accepted prime Weil theorem. Higher even exponents use square-zero localization and unit-root rigidity; higher odd exponents use the proved quadratic Gauss cancellation. Unequal prime valuations vanish. All frequencies are arbitrary signed integers.
