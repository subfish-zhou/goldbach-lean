    Finite rectangular partial summation #

    The coefficients in this file are completely arbitrary. In particular, arithmetic coefficients, phases, and nonrectangular support indicators belong in a, not in the weight. The difference kernel is the tensor product of forward differences, with the weight extended by zero outside the box.
