    Canonical extraction of a divisor from two positive factors #

    Fouvry (1987), p. 627, §III.5, the finite identity immediately before (3.12). Here the extracted divisor is called D; in that identity it is δ δ₂, not the three-factor phase modulus δ δ₁ δ₂. The split is determined by Δ' = gcd D s, using gcd cancellation only.
