    Logarithmic lcm-weight sum and the very-large-gcd zero mode #

    The symmetric inequality 2 |c_q c_r| ≤ c_q² + c_r² pays the two modulus weights by a single divisor moment. No submultiplicativity of tau_j at noncoprime arguments, and no arithmetic-progression input, is assumed.
