    The same-mask zero mode for a large common modulus #

    Every further submask of gcd(q,r)>Y is allowed, without symmetry or positivity assumptions on the mask or coefficients. Absolute values are enlarged to the full symmetric congruence relation before applying the quadratic energy bound. The exact lcm denominator is retained throughout.

    This bounds the actual masked zero mode, not a centered covariance or Siegel--Walfisz cancellation restricted to a mask. The original progression sum, nonzero modes, and their signed-error composition are separate results.
