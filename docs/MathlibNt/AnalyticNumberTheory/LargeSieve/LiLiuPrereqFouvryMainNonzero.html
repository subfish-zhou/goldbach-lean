    A common-index and r mean in the main nonzero branch #

    Fouvry (1987), p. 632, (4.8). The constant term, not the slope, is nonzero when h'*s ≠ h*s'. The r mean removes the whole r gcd cost. The subsequent common-index mean uses that constant term. The remaining s*s' gcd and divisor weight are retained explicitly, not bounded by a power of the scale and silently absorbed into an epsilon loss.
