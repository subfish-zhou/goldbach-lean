    A common smooth main term for the signed U and V dispersion sums #

    The restricted Möbius density is evaluated by a finite prime-factor argument. Consequently the two actual dispersion sums have the same zero mode. Their errors are bounded by explicit coefficient envelopes, with constants chosen before the scale, supports, coefficients, moduli, and residue. No growth bound for those envelopes and no well-factorability hypothesis is asserted here.
