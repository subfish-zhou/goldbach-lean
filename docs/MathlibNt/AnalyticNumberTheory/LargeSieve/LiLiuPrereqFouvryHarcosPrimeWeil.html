    The odd-prime Kloosterman bound #

    The polynomial character, its quadratic generating series, the finite Euler identity, the actual extension trace identity, and the Stepanov point bound are all proved in the imported modules. Cesaro averaging converts the bound on the total root power sum into individual bounds without losing multiplicity.
