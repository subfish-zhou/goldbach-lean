    Removing the actual outer coefficients before the IV.3 Gram expansion #

    The first modulus coefficient, gamma and first beta depend only on the shared outer triple. Their exact L2 cost is outside the correlation sum. The remaining signed coefficient depends only on (n₂,s'), not on k₁. The small-root phase and all carrier restrictions are still retained.
