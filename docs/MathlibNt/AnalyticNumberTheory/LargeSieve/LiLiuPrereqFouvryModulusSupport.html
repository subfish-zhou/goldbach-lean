    Sparse modulus support and inverse-lcm mass #

    The canonical supported parts δ₁,δ₂ force a large square divisor in the corresponding modulus. A gcd harmonic row estimate and three fixed-order divisor bounds preserve this saving for signed inverse-lcm weights.
