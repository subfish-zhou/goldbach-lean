    Large common-modulus payment at the C.2 dyadic scale #

    The actual beta endpoint is 2 * T when 4 * M * T = x. Both lengths have a positive power lower bound with exponent min ε (min η (1 / 2)). The original sum, zero mode and tail use one identical arbitrary mask, and the frequency cutoff is exactly wUniformCutoff M (x ^ η) throughout.
