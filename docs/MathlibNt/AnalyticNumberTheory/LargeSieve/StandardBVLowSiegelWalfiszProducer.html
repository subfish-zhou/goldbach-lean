    Producer for the low-conductor Standard-BV source #

    This module reduces the low source to two genuinely one-dimensional inputs: the already isolated modulus-one PNT/partial-summation contract, and a pointwise primitive nonprincipal ψ(N,χ) Siegel--Walfisz estimate. All finite character multiplicities, conductor fibres and logarithmic losses are paid here.
