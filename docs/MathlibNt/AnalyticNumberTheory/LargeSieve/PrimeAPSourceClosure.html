    Elementary source closure for prime-AP partial summation #

    This module discharges the higher-prime-power term by a literal finite support count. It also records the minimal one-dimensional hypotheses needed for the global PNT and Chebyshev-to-li sources. No AP or Bombieri--Vinogradov conclusion is assumed in either source contract.
