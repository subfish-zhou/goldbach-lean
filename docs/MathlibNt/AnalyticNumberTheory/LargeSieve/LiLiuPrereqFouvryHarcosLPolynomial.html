    Actual coefficient sums for Harcos's L-polynomial #

    The monic polynomials are parametrized by all their lower coefficients, not by an assumed list of L-function coefficients. Source: Harcos, §3, Theorem 5, pages/harcos-lpolynomial-06.png.
