    A constructed frequency cutoff and uniform masked tail bound #

    Choosing H(q,r)=ceil(lcm(q,r)*Z/M) makes the dimensionless frequency cutoff at least Z for every modulus pair. The remaining coefficient envelope is evaluated by the proved global fixed-order divisor means.
