    Quantitative six-key grouping of the actual extracted exponential sum #

    The five canonical gcd coordinates and the extraction divisor Δ give an explicit finite box. The complementary divisor Δ' is determined on each fiber. All original masks, cutoffs, signed coefficients, and both frequency signs remain in the actual fiber sums. The triangle inequality is used only between these fibers, not between their oscillatory summands.

    This supplies the finite-key cost underlying Fouvry (1987), p. 627, (3.12). It is not an IV.3 cancellation estimate or a completion of C.2.
