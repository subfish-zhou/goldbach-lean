    Wu's real product endpoints #

    Wu (2004), printed p. 220, defines the prime count with m * p ≤ y and li(t) = ∫₂ᵗ du / log u. The frozen Pan count has a natural endpoint. These bridges retain the real argument of li and the coupled inverse residue.

    The integral below is a total Lean expression. Analytic uses in this module require its argument to be at least 2; no convention across the singularity at 1 is inferred from totalization.
