    The complete induced-principal contribution in Wu's weighted norm #

    The prime count removes primes dividing the modulus. The frozen elementary deletion bound and weighted reciprocal-totient estimate pay for this removal, uniformly even over modulus-dependent coefficient and endpoint choices. No nonprincipal-character estimate is asserted here.
