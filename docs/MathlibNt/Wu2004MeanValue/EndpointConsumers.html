    Real moving endpoints and the manuscript's prime-endpoint domain #

    The natural product profile a * floor(r(a)) exactly retains every prime at the real endpoint r(a). On every nonempty manuscript block both prime endpoints are at least the source prime, hence at least 2. No extension of the logarithmic integral below 2 is used.
