    Open product intervals and the upper-endpoint atom #

    The frozen manuscript's B_H has two open endpoints. Wu's prime count on printed p. 220 instead has a closed upper endpoint. Subtracting two Wu counts therefore requires removal of one possible upper-endpoint prime, not an unqualified equality with the open interval.
