    Actual AP errors at a common real moving profile #

    The finite character and cofactor algebra of Pan--Wang--Ding (1975), p. 601, (2.4), is applied to the actual Wu count, not to a surrogate main term. The profile and coefficients are common across moduli; the reduced residue may be chosen separately for each modulus, but not for each source coordinate.
