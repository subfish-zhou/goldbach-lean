    Uniform prime-counting error at coefficient-dependent real endpoints #

    This is the untwisted principal core, not the nonprincipal character estimate in Wu (2004), Lemma 2.3. It consumes the frozen, proved Standard BV theorem through its modulus-one term. All constants precede the moving endpoints. Wu's integral normalization is used exactly, on the domain [2, infinity).
