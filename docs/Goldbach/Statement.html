    The literal statement of Chen's theorem #

    This specification uses only natural-number primality, parity, addition, and multiplication. It does not use any project-specific almost-prime predicate. The two factors in the composite case are allowed to coincide.

    def Goldbach.ChenTheorem :
    Prop

    Every sufficiently large even natural number is a prime plus either a prime or a product of two primes. The threshold is existential, not explicit.
